Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how the Stickle app for iPhone and Android ("Stickle", "the app") handles information. Stickle is made by Built with Paws ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email matt.white29@icloud.com.

In short: Stickle has no account, no sign-up and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The only third parties involved are Google's advertising service, which shows the ads that keep Stickle free, and the leaderboard service built into your phone (Apple Game Center or Google Play Games) if you choose to use it.

Information stored on your device

Stickle saves your game progress and settings on your device only: levels solved and best moves, Daily puzzle results and streaks, Rush best score, Stickle Zoo progress and stickers, and your choices such as sound, music, language and reminders. We never receive this information. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stickle shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and used by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region where consent is required, Stickle shows Google's consent form when you first open the app, and you can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy choices in the game's Settings. On iPhone, Stickle asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking, and you can change this in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Leaderboards (Game Center and Google Play Games)

Rush mode has an optional leaderboard. On iPhone this uses Apple Game Center, and on Android it uses Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in, your best Rush score is sent to that service along with your player name or ID so it can appear on the leaderboard. This is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). You can sign out of Game Center or Play Games in your device's settings or the Play Games app.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, Stickle schedules reminders (for example, that a new Daily puzzle is ready) directly on your device. They are created by the app on your phone, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or your phone's settings.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share or Copy on a Daily result, Stickle prepares a short text of coloured squares showing your moves. It is only shared where and when you choose.

What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell your personal information. Stickle has no in-app purchases and no account.

Children

Stickle is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Information collected by Google or Apple can be managed through the links above and your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state privacy laws, and you can contact us with any request or question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Stickle handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
