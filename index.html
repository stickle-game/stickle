About Stickle

Stickle is a sliding block puzzle game. Swipe to move every block at once, and blocks of the same colour stick together. Join each colour into one piece to solve the puzzle.

It has 700 puzzles across 70 worlds, a new Daily puzzle every day, a timed Rush mode and the Stickle Zoo. Stickle is free to play, supported by ads, with no in-app purchases.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, as there is no account or online save.

How do I change my ad choices? Open Settings in the game and tap Ad privacy choices (shown where it applies to your region).

How do I turn off reminders? In the game's Settings, or in your phone's notification settings.
